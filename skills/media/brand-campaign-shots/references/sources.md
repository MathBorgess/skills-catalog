# Sources

The triptych format is the technique of one public post by [@AmirMushich](https://x.com/AmirMushich), rewritten as a format spec and an inspection list. The original prompt is his; read it at the link.

| Part | Post | What was taken |
|---|---|---|
| Triptych panels, styling push, technical look | [Hi-end photo campaigns for any brand](https://x.com/AmirMushich/status/2007890867626057901) | Three stacked panels (portrait, gesture, macro texture), a muse derived from the brand's archetype, avant-garde styling with a deliberate oddity, hyper-real photographic look |

The Brand DNA brief, the user-correctable casting and the logo inspection are additions of this catalog. The source asks the model to analyze the brand and cast the muse inside the prompt; here that decision is written down before generation.
