# Voice and cadence

Rules for filling Block 2 (`Voice DNA`) of `brand-dna.md` and enforcing human-grade writing across scripts, captions, and posts.

## Burstiness and rhythm

AI text flattens sentence lengths into a uniform 12–16 word band. Human editorial voice oscillates deliberately:

- **Sentence oscillation.** Pair short, high-impact sentences (`<= 5` words) with longer structural sentences (`20–28` words) that carry mechanism, proof, or narrative tension.
- **Spoken beat ceiling.** For narrated or talking-head video (`social-video-director`), a single spoken sentence in a beat stays at or under `18` words so a listener never loses the thread and burned captions fit cleanly.
- **High-entropy breaks.** Break lines, slides, or beats right before the surprising noun, number, or failure, never on filler prepositions.

## Specificity over abstraction

- Replace vague adjectives ("revolutionary", "powerful", "seamless", "game-changer") with observable facts: a number, a file name, a failure mode, a concrete time cost, or a direct comparison.
- Separate **fact** (observed or measured), **opinion** (authored stance), **hypothesis** (untested expectation), and **staging** (deliberate dramatization). Never invent metrics, client wins, or quotes.

## Default Banned AI Clichés

Include these in `Banned AI Clichés` unless the author explicitly overrides them, plus any brand-specific bans:

- **Antithesis crutch**: `"It's not X, it's Y"` / `"Não é sobre X, é sobre Y"` / `"Mais do que X, é Y"`. State what the thing is directly.
- **Em-dash overuse**: chaining em-dashes (`—`) as a substitute for period or colon structure.
- **Hollow openers**: `"In today's fast-paced world"`, `"Let's dive in"`, `"Unpopular opinion:"`, `"Here's the truth:"`, or greeting filler (`"Hey guys"`, `"Oi pessoal, tudo bem?"`) in the first 3 seconds of a short video.
- **Triadic corporate filler**: three abstract nouns or adjectives in a row (`"scalable, robust, and intuitive"`).
