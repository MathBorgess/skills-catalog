# Provenance and reuse terms

This operator adapts a morphological project exploration into a reusable decision workflow. Its commercial-design instructions adapt **Revenue-Centric Design**, attributed to **Richard ([@richardrx](https://x.com/richardrx))**, from the [source repository at commit 1942760c2242f609bf63ce2c97204f5a1791bd68](https://github.com/heliocosta-dev/revenue-centric-design/tree/1942760c2242f609bf63ce2c97204f5a1791bd68). Catalog adaptation: Matheus Borges.

## Material used

Load only the local review needed for this workflow. The source references behind the adaptation are:

- [Source entrypoint](https://github.com/heliocosta-dev/revenue-centric-design/blob/1942760c2242f609bf63ce2c97204f5a1791bd68/SKILL.md): buyer and business value together, evidence before promises, differentiation.
- [Positioning, ICP and go-to-market](https://github.com/heliocosta-dev/revenue-centric-design/blob/1942760c2242f609bf63ce2c97204f5a1791bd68/references/positioning-icp-and-gtm.md): a specific buyer, meaningful pain and a reachable acquisition channel.
- [Pricing and monetization](https://github.com/heliocosta-dev/revenue-centric-design/blob/1942760c2242f609bf63ce2c97204f5a1791bd68/references/pricing-and-monetization.md): cost and value boundaries, acquisition costs and a reason to pay.
- [Metrics and experimentation](https://github.com/heliocosta-dev/revenue-centric-design/blob/1942760c2242f609bf63ce2c97204f5a1791bd68/references/metrics-and-experimentation.md): meaningful actions and the limits of weak signals.

The [preserved source exploration](https://github.com/MathBorgess/projeto-ferramenta-criacomp/tree/f7d3ef162595d01fd8ec685422e6d264cc658282/docs/experimentos/2026-10-19-sorteio-rcd) supplies procedural lineage: sampled paths, isolated proponents, an independent critic and preserved outputs. It does not validate commercial demand. The reusable operator adds a revision of every candidate and reselection after competitor evidence; those additions must not be described as steps already performed in that source run. Project-specific proposals, prices and personal inputs are not templates for new runs.

## Evidence boundary

The pinned references document the method's origin, not current market facts. Do not import their numerical examples as benchmarks or treat anecdotes as measured outcomes for a new candidate. Label assumptions, estimates, sourced claims and observations separately. Neither an agent's ranking nor a produced pitch proves demand, customer benefit, superiority or realized revenue.
