# HyperFrames handoff

Load [production MANDATES](production-mandates.md) and include project-local `MANDATES.md` with any recovered context. The packet targets HyperFrames. Preserve all production rules alongside its timing and dependency data.

Deliver a pinned direction packet with selected events, relationships, evidence, coverage and unknowns. Keep all behavior detail needed to rebuild the reference: typography, graphical construction, motion, audio and narrative correspondence. A markdown summary alone is insufficient when the structured events contain important timing.

The editor maps confirmed intent to BRIEF.md, scene order to STORYBOARD.md, appearance to frame.md and behavior to compositions. Use current installed HyperFrames contracts during production. Do not generate an apparently approved BRIEF before HyperFrames initialization: that file acts as a no-repeat signal for its interview. Unknown creative choices remain open.

Source and production clocks are different. Retain source timestamps as evidence and describe semantic anchors for adaptation. `data-start`, `data-duration` and `data-media-start` are distinct. `data-track-index` organizes editor lanes, not z-order. The runtime owns playback and animations must be seekable. Detailed observed movement guides implementation; it does not reveal the original author's code.

For an adaptation, show:

1. What the user wants to change and which references/revisions it uses.
2. Every affected dependency, including indirect effects on other layers.
3. Proposed resolutions and their creative consequences.
4. The exact human decision and evidence after the human responds.

Do not resolve affected dependencies silently, even if an adjustment looks obvious. Store proposed resolutions in the adaptation file before creating the packet. Only record an approval for that exact proposal. A stale revision or changed resolution requires a new proposal and decision. The CLI cannot authenticate human consent; truthful recording is the caller's responsibility.

The packet status is not a render/fidelity result. A pilot must give another agent the recovered packet, reconstruct a critical interval in HyperFrames and compare original/reconstruction for text construction, graphical order, motion and audible synchronization. Report structural checks separately from the actual comparison. Never infer editorial approval from a successful render.
