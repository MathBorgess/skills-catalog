# First-fold hook engineering (`post-hooks.md`)

Use this reference in Step 4, **after** `moodboard.md` is confirmed. The first fold is everything the reader sees before clicking *"more"* or swiping to Slide 2.

## Anatomy of a First-Fold Hook

- **For Carousels (`Slide 1`):**
  1. **Cover Headline (`2–6` words):** Large display typography (`>= 84 px` on `1080x1350`) with one emphasized word in the accent color.
  2. **Sub-hook (`<= 14` words):** Sharp secondary line that creates the open loop for Slide 2.
  3. **Visual Cue:** One unmistakable visual object, diagram, or contrast that proves the topic at a glance.
- **For Text Posts / Threads (`Lines 1–2`):**
  1. **Line 1 (`<= 12` words):** Concrete claim, failure, metric, or premise break.
  2. **Line 2 (`<= 18` words):** Raises the stakes or names the exact friction right before the fold break.

## Five psychological hook triggers (`viral-rag`)

Propose **three candidates** using three different triggers from this table, shaped by `moodboard.md`:

| Trigger | Mechanism | Example structure |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Contrarian Premise Break** | Challenges a habit the target audience practices daily | *"Most agent prompts fail because they explain too much, not too little."* |
| **2. Proof-First / Real Number or Failure** | Opens with an observed metric, diff, or incident | *"We cut 60% of our skill instructions and the pass rate went up."* |
| **3. Curiosity Gap / Diagnostic** | Names a symptom the reader recognizes but cannot yet explain | *"When an image model breaks your grid layout twice in a row, stop editing the prompt."* |
| **4. Direct Call-Out** | Filters for the exact practitioner and problem | *"If your team shares a brand kit that no AI tool actually obeys, read this."* |
| **5. Hard Operational Rule** | States a battle-tested constraint with authority | *"One job per skill. If the description needs an 'and also', it is two skills."* |
