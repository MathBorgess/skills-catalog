# Sources

The archetypes are the technique of three public posts by [@AmirMushich](https://x.com/AmirMushich), rewritten here as structure, slots and checks. The original prompts are his; read them at the links.

| Archetype | Post | What was taken |
|---|---|---|
| A. Flat digital portrait | [Modern sport poster design](https://x.com/AmirMushich/status/2000932708684304623) | Flat-surface medium statement, color container tied to the subject, two-name typographic treatment, no text over the face |
| B. Photo-grid tribute | [Photo-grid tribute poster design](https://x.com/AmirMushich/status/2008976966255337666) | Silhouette container filled by a textured mosaic, selective color overlays, type size caps as percentages of width |
| C. Ensemble from references | [Nano Banana Pro test: 8 images input](https://x.com/AmirMushich/status/1991589113976304068) | Group composition, and the finding that more reference images and a longer prompt both lowered consistency |

The catalog these posts belong to is the author's article [50 design prompts](https://x.com/AmirMushich/status/2019073081881293302).
