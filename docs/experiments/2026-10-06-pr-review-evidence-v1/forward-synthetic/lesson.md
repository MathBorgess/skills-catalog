# Failure details need one summary and visible consequences

Claim: One summary per failed check can reduce repetition while preserving evidence.
Status: hypothesis from supplied development feedback.

## Evidence

The fixture reports a failed authorization check and a wrapper success.
The packet keeps the failure visible and identifies the wrapper conflict.
A linter can check sentence rules; it cannot establish review speed or safety.

## Scope

This lesson covers a synthetic fixture and a proposal.
No baseline comparison, independent repeats, human labels, or final test supports a benefit claim.
No earlier lesson exists to supersede.

## Counterexample

Failures with one name can expose different risks.
The summary must keep each material consequence and its evidence.
