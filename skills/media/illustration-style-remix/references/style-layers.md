# Style layers

Every style is six layers. Write each as something the eye can check, and keep the anchor's name out of the layers.

| Layer | Question it answers | Example of an observable trait |
|---|---|---|
| Composition | What is framed, from what angle, with how much space? | Head only, centered, three-quarter view, generous empty space |
| Linework | How are the lines made? | Brush-pen ink, thickness varies, scratchy edges, slight ink bleed |
| Characterization | What attitude do the faces carry? | Tired eyes, wide expressive mouth, angular features |
| Palette | Which colors, and how many? | Soft pastels for hair, clothing and accents |
| Shading | How are shadows drawn? | Hard-edged cel blocks of a darker tone under nose, brow and jaw; no soft gradients |
| Background | What sits behind? | Flat pure white, plenty of air |

## Writing the layers

- Put a hard limit in the layer it belongs to and state it as a positive first: "the head floats alone in the frame" before "no neck or shoulders".
- Anchor the whole table with one line naming the era or medium; the six layers carry the look.
- A layer too vague to fail ("cool vibe") is not a layer; rewrite it until an output could clearly pass or fail it.
- Skin tone and features belong to the subject, not to the style: write "natural and faithful to the subject" in the subject slot, and leave color choices to the palette layer for hair, clothing and background.

## Worked example: 2000s virtual-band comic head

Anchor: the gritty comic look of an early-2000s animated virtual band.

| Layer | Trait |
|---|---|
| Composition | The head alone, floating in the middle of the frame, three-quarter view, with generous space around it; no neck, shoulders or body |
| Linework | Textured brush-pen ink; line weight varies; scratchy, imperfect, hand-drawn on paper |
| Characterization | Slightly cynical and tired eyes, an exaggerated mouth, angular features, an underground attitude |
| Palette | Soft pastels for hair, clothing details and accents; the subject's own natural skin tone |
| Shading | Hard-edged cel shading in distinct blocks of a darker tone; no airbrushing |
| Background | Flat pure white |
