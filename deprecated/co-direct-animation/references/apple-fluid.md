# Apple fluid design

Source: [Emil Kowalski's Apple design synthesis](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills/blob/2d228630ba1446365f614a108cb8252f1feda202/skills/apple-design/SKILL.md), inspected 2026-10-11. This is a community interpretation of Apple design talks, not an official Apple skill. Evidence level: documented practice, not a controlled test of this output.

Start with a legible focal point, deliberate type hierarchy and stable spatial relationships. Carry position and velocity continuously across motion changes; avoid unexplained jumps. Keep bounce purposeful. For interactive work, preserve immediate feedback and interruptibility. Materials should explain depth rather than decorate every surface.

For linear video, adapt these to continuous trajectories and readable reveals. Do not impose pointer gestures or runtime springs on a fixed timeline. Reduce disruptive movement in an accessible interactive variant.

Pull toward the author: keep their font, palette, approved marks, composition rules and motion settings. Apple-inspired hierarchy does not require a system font, glass cards, monochrome, or reproducing an Apple interface. If the author specifies a hard cut or elastic action, preserve it and use other compatible principles.
