# Spatial collections: explore, then refine

Build an interesting change of state before applying brand polish. Exploration produces several distinct scenes whose appeal comes from relationships, material and spatial transformation. A collection is not the same choreography recolored three times. Default to three short studies when the user requests a collection without a count; vary material, spatial organization and energy transfer.

Follow [the production workflow](production-workflow.md) and external TO-SPEC for small validation deliveries. Proposed collection size is not authorization to render every study to completion: advance selected studies through the agreed gates.

## Two stages, one source

`exploration` prioritizes content, dynamics and space. Keep the source DESIGN.md and its authorship intact, identify retained constraints, and record which styling decisions the author explicitly defers. Use neutral working forms and temporary aesthetic choices where authorized. A source snapshot is provenance, not proof of brand compliance. Never claim that exploration conforms to every token when refinement was postponed.

`refinement` takes selected scene(s) and adapts palette, typography, wording, approved assets and authored motion constraints to the original, including scoped current overrides. Preserve the scene's interesting mechanism. Record before/after decisions and repeat visual inspection. Each stage gets its own run and source snapshot; cite the exploration run and chosen studies in the refinement record. Do not silently flip a completed exploration into an approved identity.

Do not use a logo as the main object unless specifically requested for that work. The focal role can belong to an ensemble, passing impulse, unfolding surface, magnetic cluster or changing topology. During neutral exploration, omit marks entirely. Brand refinement does not imply adding a logo.

## Spatial direction

Specify a coordinate system, perspective/orthographic projection, camera path, foreground/midground/background, light direction, material response and contact/occlusion behavior. In perspective, size and parallax must agree with depth. A camera move should reveal a relation or form; it must not disguise an inactive scene. Keep a stable reference plane or architectural cue when useful.

Actual 3D or carefully staged 2.5D can work; label the chosen technique honestly. Use at least three mutually consistent depth cues: parallax, occlusion, projected scale, perspective, cast/contact shadows, shading, atmospheric separation or focus. Do not claim physically accurate rendering for painted shadows. Avoid a flat card grid decorated with arbitrary blur as the default answer to a spatial brief.

Alive means responsive: neighbors yield, fields propagate through material, lighter parts follow heavier parts, compression stores energy, recovery changes the arrangement. Separate primary group movement, secondary elastic response and quiet environmental motion. Give negative space and rest a role.

## Collection record

For each study, record title, viewer emotion, central event, cast, material, depth system, causal score, final readable pose, duration, output and what distinguishes it from its siblings. Evaluate interest with concrete observations: is there a readable transformation, a surprising but motivated turn, spatial continuity and a satisfying consequence? Record visual judgment separately from measured technical checks. Do not invent an objective “creativity score.”

Useful directions include elastic constellations gathering and releasing, hinged surfaces passing a wave, and weighted modules receiving an impulse and organizing into a structure. They are examples, not required templates. Look for new verbs and relationships in future collections.

## Delivery and refinement handoff

Deliver each playable study, a contact sheet and a comparison describing behavior and remaining limitations. Record pending author decisions in `## Authorship refinement`; distinguish “deferred by author” from missing information. Save all retained experiment videos to the user's requested archive when that destination is authorized; verify the account, folder, names, sizes and readback. Record returned IDs/URLs privately in the run, never in the public skill. Do not upload client assets just because a video upload is authorized.
