# Sources

The six-layer decomposition is the technique of one public post by [@AmirMushich](https://x.com/AmirMushich), generalized from its single style to any style. The original prompt is his; read it at the link.

| Part | Post | What was taken |
|---|---|---|
| Layer structure and the worked example | [2000's Gorillaz-styled illustrations](https://x.com/AmirMushich/status/2012607112766018019) | A prompt built from composition, linework, characterization, palette, shading and background; a hard limit stated inside the composition layer; natural skin tone kept respectful to the subject |

The style block, the probe-then-set procedure and the drift check are additions of this catalog.
