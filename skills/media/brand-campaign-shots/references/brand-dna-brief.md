# Brand DNA brief

One file, `brand-dna.md`, one line per field. Mark each line `[known]` with its source, or `[inferred]` with the observation behind it.

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| Archetype | The brand's character in one phrase (the rebel, the craftsman, the caretaker) |
| Philosophy | What the brand believes, in one sentence |
| Audience lifestyle | Who buys, how they live, where they are seen |
| Palette | 3–5 colors with hex values; the brand's signature first |
| Materials and textures | What its products and spaces are made of or feel like |
| Tone | Three words |
| Never | Two or three things the brand would not be seen doing or showing |
| Visual lineage | The photographic or art-direction tradition the brand sits closest to |

## Rules

- A `[known]` line cites a user statement, a project file or a public brand source; an uncited line is `[inferred]`.
- A field with nothing to go on stays `[inferred]` with the plain reason ("no public statement found"); a plausible invention marked `[known]` is a defect.
- The palette feeds the color grade in the prompt, and the Never line feeds the inspection.
